Our Story

Founded on Compassion. Built by Dedication.

"Access fails at the bedside, in a single moment, when a family has to choose between treatment and survival."

Good Shepherd Mirest Hospital was founded by Dr. Oluebube "Miracle" Okoye, a Medical Doctor and Pharmacist with over 12 years of healthcare experience in Nigeria. Dr. Okoye's journey to founding this hospital began with a moment that changed his life forever—treating a severely malnourished three-year-old boy whose family could not afford hospital admission.

Witnessing that impossible choice—between a child's life and a family's financial survival—opened Dr. Okoye's eyes to a deeper truth: medical expertise alone isn't enough. Healthcare access had to reach everyone, including those with the least resources.

Professional Background

With over 12 years of healthcare experience in Nigeria, Dr. Okoye brings both medical and pharmaceutical expertise to his practice. His dual qualification as a Medical Doctor and Pharmacist, combined with his deep commitment to patient care, inspired him to build Good Shepherd Mirest Hospital—a place where medical expertise meets compassion, and where no family is turned away because they lack money.

Mirest Foundation

Ensuring Healthcare Access for Those in Financial Hardship

🤝 Our Commitment

Founded by Dr. Okoye, the Mirest Foundation ensures patients with severe financial constraints can access quality healthcare without burden. No one is turned away because they lack money.

✓ What We Provide

Free consultations • Subsidized or free treatment • Emergency care support • Long-term care for chronic conditions • Free maternity services for those in need

🌍 Strategic Partnerships

We partner with foundations, organizations, and hospitals to expand healthcare access. Together, we help more families in our communities.

How the Mirest Foundation Works

If you or your family cannot afford care, we have solutions. Through the Mirest Foundation, you can access:

  • Free or subsidized consultations
  • Reduced-cost treatment programs
  • Emergency care with flexible payment
  • Support for chronic condition management
  • Complete maternity care at no cost

Our Mission

Good Shepherd Mirest Hospital exists to provide compassionate, quality, and affordable healthcare for mothers, children, and families in Ekwulobia and beyond.

We believe that access to excellent medical care should not depend on a family's wealth. Through our commitment to affordability, the Mirest Foundation, and strategic partnerships, we ensure that quality healthcare reaches everyone—especially those with the fewest resources.

Every patient who walks through our doors is treated with dignity and respect. Because for us, healthcare is not a business—it's a calling.
