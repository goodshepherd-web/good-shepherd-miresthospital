Our Services

Comprehensive healthcare for mothers, children, and families

What We Offer

Complete healthcare services with compassion and expertise

👶

Pediatrics

Specialized, compassionate care for children from newborns to adolescents. We've successfully managed over 2,600 babies and children in the past 5 years with excellent outcomes.

Includes:

  • Newborn care and check-ups
  • Vaccinations and immunizations
  • Growth and development monitoring
  • Treatment of childhood illnesses
  • Nutritional counseling
  • Expert pediatric care with proven track record
🤰

Maternity & Antenatal

Complete maternity care from pregnancy planning through postpartum support.

Includes:

  • Pregnancy monitoring and ultrasounds
  • Antenatal education classes
  • Safe delivery services
  • Postnatal care and support
  • Free services for those in need
🚨

Emergency Services

24/7 emergency care with immediate response for urgent medical situations.

Available:

  • 24-hour emergency response
  • Trauma and injury care
  • Acute illness treatment
  • Rapid assessment team
  • Flexible emergency payments
🔬

Laboratory Services

Complete laboratory testing with fast, accurate results and diagnostics.

Includes:

  • Blood tests and screening
  • Imaging and ultrasound
  • Diagnostic testing
  • Quick turnaround results
  • Affordable pricing
💊

Pharmacy Services

On-site pharmacy with full medication range and expert guidance.

Includes:

  • Wide range of medications
  • Pharmacist consultations
  • Medication guidance
  • Affordable drug prices
  • Prescription fulfillment
🏥

General Medicine

Consultation and treatment for general health concerns and family care.

Includes:

  • General consultations
  • Chronic disease management
  • Health screening
  • Preventive care
  • Treatment for common illnesses
