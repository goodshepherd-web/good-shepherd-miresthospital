Founder's Vision

The Good Shepherd Mirest Story

Dr. Oluebube "Miracle" Okoye founded Good Shepherd Mirest Hospital with a simple belief: healthcare access should never be limited by financial constraints.

As both a Medical Doctor and Registered Pharmacist with over 12 years of experience in healthcare, Dr. Okoye saw too many families struggling to afford quality care. She decided to create something different—a hospital that combines expert medical care with compassion and affordability.

Since founding Good Shepherd Mirest Hospital, Dr. Okoye and her dedicated team have cared for over 10,000 children and served 2,600+ families. Every child, every family, receives the same expert care and compassion.

"I stayed humble without becoming small." — Dr. Oluebube Okoye

2026 Recognition: PracticeLink First Practice Fund recipient — 1 of 9 selected globally for healthcare excellence and impact.

Mirest Foundation — Healthcare for All

The Mirest Foundation was created to ensure that no family is turned away because they cannot afford healthcare. We believe quality medical care is a right, not a privilege.

What We Do:

  • ✓ Free consultations for families in need
  • ✓ Subsidized treatment programs
  • ✓ Emergency care for vulnerable populations
  • ✓ Maternal and child health initiatives
  • ✓ Health education programs
  • ✓ Community outreach and wellness
