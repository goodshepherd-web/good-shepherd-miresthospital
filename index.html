Our Services

Comprehensive healthcare for mothers, children, and families

👶 Pediatrics

Specialized care for children from newborns to adolescents. Well-child checks, vaccinations, and treatment of childhood illnesses.

🤰 Maternity

Complete maternity services including antenatal care, safe delivery, and postnatal support. Free maternity program available.

🚨 Emergency Care

24/7 emergency services for urgent medical conditions. Immediate response team available at all times.

🔬 Laboratory

Complete laboratory testing services with quick results. Blood work, imaging, and specialized diagnostics.

💊 Pharmacy

On-site pharmacy with wide range of medications. Qualified pharmacists available for medication guidance.

🏥 General Medicine

Consultation and treatment for general health concerns. Experienced medical team for your family's needs.
