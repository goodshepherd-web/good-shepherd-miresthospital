Our Impact

Since our founding in 2021, Good Shepherd Mirest Hospital has been driven by a single mission: to provide compassionate, accessible, affordable healthcare for mothers, children, and families in Ekwulobia and beyond.

"I stayed humble without becoming small." — Dr. Pharm Oluebube Okoye, Founder

These words capture our commitment: we maintain our values while refusing to compromise on excellence. Every patient receives our full attention, every family receives our full heart.

Your support helps us:

  • ✓ Provide free or subsidized care to families in financial need
  • ✓ Maintain 24/7 emergency services
  • ✓ Offer comprehensive maternity and pediatric care
  • ✓ Train and support our medical team
  • ✓ Purchase and maintain medical equipment
  • ✓ Support chronic condition management programs

Mirest Foundation

Our commitment to giving back extends beyond the hospital

🏥 Free Consultations

We provide free medical consultations to families who cannot afford basic healthcare.

🤰 Free Maternity Services

No mother should face the choice between her health and her family's financial security. We offer free maternity care to those in need.

💊 Chronic Care Support

Ongoing support for patients managing chronic conditions ensures they don't fall through the cracks.

🆘 Emergency Relief

Emergency situations should never be delayed by financial concerns. We provide emergency care regardless of ability to pay.

🤝 Inter-Hospital Support

We partner with other hospitals and organizations to expand our reach and impact across communities.

📚 Health Education

We provide health education and preventive care resources to promote community wellness.
